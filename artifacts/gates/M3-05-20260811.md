## Security and portability review

The planner snapshots attempt history and durable records, and context preparation occurs
before any route hash. Serializable SQLite reads return one exact installation, agent, and
provider set. Exact expected versions, `Ready`, primary-profile model, catalog/profile identity,
persisted capability flags, and agent input/output/tool/time budgets fail closed before health.
A model string cannot select outside the current primary-model policy.

Health records are unique and capped at 256. Status/source, failure count, safe evidence code,
observation, expiry, and retry time are typed and bounded; evidence lives at most 15 minutes.
Missing, future, expired, unknown, unavailable, and already-attempted profiles are exclusions.
Removing a failure record therefore cannot make a profile healthy. Attempt history is capped at
eight unique exact-catalog profiles. No remote error body is accepted or returned.

After routing, authority and relevant health are read again and eligibility repeats. Authority
changes conflict; health/eligibility changes return a retryable external failure. Successful
plans expire in at most five seconds and expose only versions, typed selection, and prepared
input/context/health/plan hashes—not request content, endpoint, secret reference, or credential.
The implementation passes identically on Windows and Ubuntu and uses `IClock` for selection time.

## Remaining findings

- The production health source is intentionally empty. No durable observation writer, circuit
  breaker, or external health probe is authorized by this gate.
- A route plan is not authorization and is not persisted. Configuration or destination state can
  change after planning; the consuming run service must atomically revalidate it.
- Durable model run/attempt idempotency, audit, cumulative token/cost reservation, usage
  reconciliation, cancellation, stop conditions, and immutable run snapshots remain closed.
- Current durable provider profiles authorize text, streaming, tools, and images. Structured,
  audio, and document authority remains unavailable and fails typed until its schema/gate exists.
- Endpoint classification, DNS/IP revalidation, actual failover execution, vendor adapters, and
  credential materialization through a selected plan remain future gates.
- No public API/CLI model route exists, and both production catalogs remain empty.

## Rollback

Remove the health/authority/planner records and Abstractions; remove the immutable health catalog,
route planner, widened bounded exclusion limit, and DI registrations; remove the serializable
Persistence reader and its tests; remove ADR 0023, ledger/architecture/threat/runbook/project-state
updates, and this report. There is no migration, package, stored health record, credential,
provider call, public route, or external artifact to reverse. Keep both production catalogs empty.
